# Ready-made style sentences

Copy **style** into the spec's `style` field; append the **character** (or vehicle / weapon) sentence to the matching subject's `desc` as ", <sentence>"; the **setting materials** sentence can be appended to `setting`. Styles marked *reliable* re-skin the world or use a painterly medium and work well with the current model; *hard* ones are flat 2D media that often come out as a tinted 3D render.

## ghibli (reliable)

- **style**: a Studio Ghibli hand-painted anime film: soft painterly gouache backgrounds with lush detail, gentle hand-drawn linework, flat warm cel shading, drifting clouds and a nostalgic, tender palette, like a frame from Spirited Away or Princess Mononoke
- **character**: redrawn as a hand-drawn Ghibli anime character: simple rounded face with small dot eyes, flat two-tone cel shading, thin clean outlines, natural proportions, dressed in simple countryside clothes (linen shirt, apron or short cloak, cloth cap) in the same identity colours
- **vehicle**: redrawn as a charming hand-painted Ghibli vehicle: rounded vintage shapes, painted metal, visible hand-drawn lines, puffs of hand-drawn smoke
- **first-person weapon**: redrawn as a hand-painted Ghibli prop: a simple cel-shaded hunting rifle or wooden staff held in hand-drawn hands, flat colours and thin outlines
- **setting materials**: the whole setting is a hand-painted Ghibli background: painterly gouache textures, soft edges, lush grass and clouds, no photoreal materials and no 3D render look
